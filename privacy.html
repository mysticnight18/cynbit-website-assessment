Privacy notice

Website inquiry form · Last updated 2 November 2026

This notice explains how information entered into the contact form on this prototype Cynbit website is handled. It applies to this website and its inquiry form only. It does not describe the practices of any separate Cynbit service or product.

Information you provide

The form asks for your name, email address, area of interest and a short project description. Company name is optional. The server also records the date and time an inquiry is received. Please do not include passwords, payment details, government identifiers or other sensitive information in the message.

Why it is used

The information is used to receive and respond to the inquiry you submit. It is not used by this prototype to build advertising profiles or sent to an email marketing service.

Where it is stored

In this prototype, submitted inquiries are appended to a JSON file on the server that runs the website. The file is not available through a public website route. A production deployment should replace this local file with an access-controlled database, define a retention schedule and configure secure backups before collecting real inquiries.

Sharing and retention

This prototype does not automatically email, sell or share inquiry details with another service. The information remains in the server’s inquiry file until an operator removes it. The local demonstration has no automated deletion schedule.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit the form. For a question about a submission, contact opportunities@cynbit.in and include enough context for the team to identify it. Avoid sending additional sensitive information by email.

Security and changes

The prototype includes basic input validation and request throttling, but a local demonstration is not a guarantee of production security. Before public launch, the operator should review hosting security, access controls, encryption, retention, incident handling and applicable legal requirements. If this notice changes, the revised version will appear on this page with a new update date.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to opportunities@cynbit.in.
